FAQ

Questions about SymbiosAlign

What is SymbiosAlign?

SymbiosAlign is a 12-week, physician-led weight loss program at Symbios Health on Hilton Head Island. It combines medical oversight, nutrition, movement, body composition tracking, medication when appropriate, and accountability into one plan built around your health.

Is SymbiosAlign only about weight loss?

No. Weight loss is one result. You and your team also track energy, strength, mobility, labs, metabolic markers, and eating habits. Most patients come to lose weight and stay because they feel stronger and more in control.

Does the program include weight loss medication?

Yes, when it is right for you. Your physician reviews your health history, labs, and metabolic picture first. If a medication such as a GLP-1 fits your plan, it comes with nutrition, movement, and tracking. It is never the whole plan.

How long is the initial program?

The initial SymbiosAlign program runs for 12 weeks. Your team builds your plan at the start, reviews your progress at week 6, and meets with you at week 12 to plan your next step.

What happens after the initial program?

At 12 weeks, your practitioner reviews your results and goals with you. Together you choose your next step, such as another phase of SymbiosAlign or ongoing support through SymbiosCare membership.

Do I need to be a Symbios Primary Care patient?

No. Many patients start with a referral from their Symbios Primary Care provider, and others contact our team directly. Call 843-738-4800 or book time with our team online to start.

How do I know if SymbiosAlign is right for me?

Talk to our team. SymbiosAlign fits adults who do the right things and still cannot lose weight, want medical oversight, or need structure and accountability. Call 843-738-4800 or book time with our team online.
