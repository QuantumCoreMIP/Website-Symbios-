FAQ

Common questions.

What services does Symbios Health offer?

Symbios Health offers primary care through PrimaryCare, medically guided weight loss and metabolic health through SymbiosAlign, fitness and mobility support through SymbiosFit, physiotherapy and lymphedema recovery through PhysioTherapy, and regenerative aesthetic care through SymbiosAria. All in one connected campus.

Instead of managing each part of your health separately, you have a team that sees the bigger picture and helps you understand what to do next. Symbios serves Hilton Head, Bluffton, and the Lowcountry.

How is Symbios different from a weight loss clinic or a gym?

A weight loss clinic focuses on your weight. A gym focuses on your workout. Symbios looks at your health history, labs, metabolism, hormones, movement, strength, and body composition together, then builds one plan from all of it.

Your physician, your trainer, and your health and behavior coach work from the same picture of your health, so you are not left connecting everything yourself.

Do I have to use every service?

No. Most patients start with the care that addresses what matters most to them right now. If another Symbios service supports your goal, your team will explain why and walk you through the options. The decision stays yours.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Symbios Health is accepting new patients in Hilton Head, Bluffton, and the surrounding Lowcountry. Call 843-738-4800 or book time with our team online.

Do you offer weight loss medication such as GLP-1?

Yes, when it is right for you, and only inside the SymbiosAlign program. Symbios does not prescribe GLP-1 medication on its own.

Your physician reviews your health history, labs, and metabolic picture first. If medication belongs in your plan, it comes with the rest of Align: nutrition, strength work, body composition tracking, and a team watching your progress and adjusting as you go.

Medication alone is not a plan. The support around it is what helps your results hold.

How do I get started?

Talk to our team. Call 843-738-4800 or book time with our team online. You will leave with a better understanding of your options and a clear next step.
