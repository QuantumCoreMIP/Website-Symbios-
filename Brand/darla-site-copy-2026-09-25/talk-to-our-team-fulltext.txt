THE PROCESS
How Getting
Started Works

This is not rushed care. It is a relationship built to
help you feel better, move better, and keep
living the life you want.

01/Schedule an Appointment

Call us or complete the form. You meet with Dr. Luther first. He listens to your goals and health history, then shows you where to start.

02/Choose the Right Path

Our team helps you choose your next step without pressure, so you know what you need.

03/Move Forward with a Clear Plan

You leave with a physician-guided care plan and a coordinated team behind it. Your providers work together, so you do not manage the details alone. You get clear answers and ongoing support from people who know you.
