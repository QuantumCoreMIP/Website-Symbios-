FAQ

Common questions.

What is Symbios Health?

Symbios Health is a physician-led practice in Hilton Head, SC, offering relationship-based primary care, medically guided weight loss, fitness and vitality support, physical therapy, and aesthetic and regenerative care. Dr. Stephen Luther, MD, founded the practice and has practiced medicine since 1992. Symbios comes from the Greek words for together and life. The mission is to do life together with patients.

What is SymbiosAlign?

SymbiosAlign is a physician-led weight loss program that combines medical evaluation, 3D body composition scanning, personal training, nutrition and health coaching, and medication management, including GLP-1 support when appropriate. Dr. Luther reviews every patient’s progress daily. It is built for people who have tried to lose weight and want a medically guided, personal plan.

Does Symbios Health offer GLP-1 medication?

Yes. GLP-1 medication is available through SymbiosAlign when medically appropriate. Dr. Stephen Luther supervises all medication management. GLP-1 support is one part of a plan that includes nutrition coaching, personal training, and regular progress reviews.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, we are accepting new patients. Contact our team through the Talk to Our Team page or call us at 843-738-4800.

Where is Symbios Health located?

Symbios Health is at 460 William Hilton Parkway, Hilton Head Island, SC 29926. We serve patients from Hilton Head, Bluffton, Sun City, and the surrounding Lowcountry.

What makes Symbios Health different?

Three things. Dr. Luther personally reviews every patient’s progress every day. Your plan is built from your labs, your body composition scan, and your goals. Your care team (physician, health coaches, dietitians, trainers, and nurse) meets weekly and coordinates your care, so you do not have to.
